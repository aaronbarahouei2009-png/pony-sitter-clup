💙 Best Friends

What friendship means to us

Best Friends

Having a best friend can make every day more fun. A good friend is someone who listens to you, makes you laugh, and stays by your side when things get difficult.

At school, friends can play games together, share jokes, and help each other with their work. Sometimes friends disagree, but they can talk about their problems and say sorry. Being a good friend means being kind, including others, and treating people with respect.

The best thing about friendship is that you don't have to be exactly the same. Friends can have different interests and still enjoy spending time together. What matters most is caring about each other and making happy memories together.
