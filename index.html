🤎 Buckskin Horses

A coat colour with golden body + dark points

🐴 Buckskin Horse Facts

1

🤎 Golden coat

Buckskins usually have a golden or tan body, with darker points including the mane, tail and lower legs.

2

🧬 A coat colour, not a breed

Buckskin describes colour, so horses from many different breeds can be buckskin.

3

🖤 Dark points

The darker mane, tail and ears are typical buckskin points; lower legs are often dark too.

4

☀️ Different from dun

Dun horses have dun-factor or “primitive” markings; a buckskin does not.

🐴 Real Horse Care Details

Simple facts from Australian animal-care guidance

💧

Fresh water

Horses need clean drinking water available at all times. Water demand rises in hot weather.

🌾

Forage matters

Good-quality roughage such as pasture or hay provides important fibre for a horse’s diet.

🦶

Hoof care

Agriculture Victoria advises routine hoof trimming about every 6–8 weeks, depending on the horse and conditions.

🦷

Dental checks

Regular dental checks matter because horses’ teeth keep wearing as they chew fibrous food.

🌿 Hawkesbury Sights

Dyarubbin, Windsor, lookouts and river country

📍 Real Hawkesbury facts

Hawkesbury Regional Museum is in Windsor beside the Hawkesbury River / Dyarubbin and has free general admission.

Thompson Square, beside the museum precinct, is described by Hawkesbury City Council as Australia’s oldest surviving public square.

Heritage trails around Hawkesbury include self-guided walks and drives that can take about 1–4 hours.

💙 Best Friends

What friendship means to us

Best Friends

Having a best friend can make every day more fun. A good friend is someone who listens to you, makes you laugh, and stays by your side when things get difficult.

At school, friends can play games together, share jokes, and help each other with their work. Sometimes friends disagree, but they can talk about their problems and say sorry. Being a good friend means being kind, including others, and treating people with respect.

The best thing about friendship is that you don't have to be exactly the same. Friends can have different interests and still enjoy spending time together. What matters most is caring about each other and making happy memories together.
